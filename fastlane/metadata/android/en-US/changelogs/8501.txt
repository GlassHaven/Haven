- **AI chat.** New chat screen for AI models over OpenAI-compatible endpoints (llama-server, vLLM, CLIProxyAPI), Ollama, Anthropic, or Gemini — chosen per profile, riding the same per-profile tunnels as every other transport. Attach gallery or camera images for vision models; long-press a message to copy its text or image, one-tap copy of the last reply, paste images from the clipboard. Transcripts are ephemeral by default; a save toggle persists them, encrypted at rest.
