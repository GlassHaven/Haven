- **Google Drive and other OAuth sign-ins work on a second attempt.** Authenticating an rclone remote binds a loopback server on the fixed redirect port, which was only released when the sign-in call finished. Cancelling or timing out a sign-in left it bound, so the next attempt hit a stale server or a port conflict. The flow is now stopped on cancel, on timeout, and before a new attempt, freeing the port (#697, thanks UlyssesZh).
